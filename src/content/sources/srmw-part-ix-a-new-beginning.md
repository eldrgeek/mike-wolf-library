<p class="display display-xl">If at first you don’t succeed,<br>lie.</p>

<h3 id="new-chapter-1-a-new-beginning"><span class="pg" id="page-133" data-page="133" aria-hidden="true"></span>New Chapter 1: A New Beginning</h3>

<p>The antimuse sat in the first paragraph of new Chapter 1, waiting for the author to arrive.</p>

<p>She waited, patiently for an hour, then moved to the second paragraph. The author did not arrive.</p>

<p>Then she waited, impatiently in the third, for another hour.</p>

<p>Then angrily in the fourth, for an hour.</p>

<p>Then furiously in the fifth, for an hour.</p>

<p>“Fuck him!” She said. She scribbled a note and stuck it near the bottom of Chapter 1:</p>

<p class="note-script">If you want your novel to not be crap, meet<br>me in New Chapter 2, tomorrow, 8:00 AM.<br>If you’re not there, I am done!!!<br>Muse</p>

<p>Then she left.</p>
