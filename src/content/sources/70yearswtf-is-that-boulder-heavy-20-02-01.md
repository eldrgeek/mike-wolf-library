This morning I listened to a conversation between [Sam Harris](https://samharris.org/about/) ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Harris)) and [Jack Kornfield](https://jackkornfield.com/) ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Kornfield)) on the [Waking Up](https://wakingup.com/) course about their experiences in practice.

Jack tells this story that I thought worth noting:

> I remember walking with [my teacher] [Ajahn Chah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ajahn_Chah) across a field one morning with our alms bowls and he points to a big boulder across the field, and he says to the monks, “Is that boulder heavy?”
>
> And you know, being Ivy League educated and all, “Of course it is.”
>
> And he said: “Not if you don’t pick it up.”

Don’t pick up things that you don’t need to carry.
