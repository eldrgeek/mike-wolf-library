<p>I started a new chapter so I’d have nothing distracting me.</p>

<p>Of all the skills that I needed, what was the first one that I needed to build?</p>

<p>What was the foundation on which I’d build the rest?</p>

<p>I didn’t know. But I was determined to figure it out.</p>

<p>I had an idea.</p>

<p>I imagined doing it in my metanovel.</p>

<p>I tried it.</p>

<p>It didn’t work.</p>

<p>But I learned something.</p>

<p>I imagined a variation.</p>

<p>I tried it.</p>

<p>It didn’t work.</p>

<p>But I learned something.</p>

<p>I had another idea.</p>

<p>I imagined doing it.</p>

<p>I tried it.</p>

<p>It didn’t work.</p>

<p>But I learned something.</p>

<p>I deleted the chapters in in which I’d imagined my attempts.</p>

<p>I wrote my practice later on.</p>
