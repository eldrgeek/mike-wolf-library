It started with a conversation at the Stonington Opera House. A young woman told me about her miracle—her child.

The next day, I asked for a miracle—and got one.

That led to me remembering a quotation I’d heard.

That led me to find the original quotation:

That led me to “A Course in Miracles.”

A few days later, I saw the young woman again in Blue Hill, met her miracle, and told her about my miracles.

There are more miracles to come.

## At the opera house

Bobbi and I were at the Stonington Opera House to see “Judy,” a movie about Judy Garland’s life. While waiting, I said something that prompted a gal behind the snack bar counter to tell me: “I had a miracle.”

“What was it?” I asked.

“I tried to have a baby and couldn’t have one. So I prayed, and I now have a baby boy. His name is Matthew. It means “a gift from God.”

“That’s wonderful,” I said. “I don’t believe in God, but I do believe in believing in God, and I’m glad you’ve got your gift.”

We went in and watched the movie. It was good.

## My request

In the mornings, I walk my driveway. It’s 500 feet up a long hill, and I walk up and down four times. It’s good exercise.

And it’s a good time to think.

As I walked the driveway the morning after “Judy,” I thought of last night’s conversation.

“I’d like a miracle,” I said to no one in particular as I started up the driveway.

I continued walking up the driveway, thinking about miracles.

And then I looked around and saw a miracle—life around me! Every bit of it seemed a miracle. My very existence, my consciousness, seemed a miracle.

“Thank you,” I said to no one in particular.

I had my miracle.

## The quotation

A few days later, I remembered something I’d heard from my friend Gil. Something like, “We are afraid of our own power.”

Google to the rescue. I found it. No great miracle there.

> Our deepest fear is not that we are inadequate.
> Our deepest fear is that we are powerful beyond measure.
> It is our light, not our darkness that most frightens us.

Marianne Williamson wrote it in her book “[A RETURN TO LOVE.](https://marianne.com/a-return-to-love/)“

I learned her book was about her experience with another book: “[A Course In Miracles](https://www.amazon.com/Course-Miracles-Dr-Helen-Schucman-ebook/dp/B0018A01L4).”

That led me to the book itself. I’ve [written about it here,](https://70yearswtf.substack.com/p/a-course-in-miracles-19-12-11) and there will be more. (Update: There have been more [here](https://70yearswtf.substack.com/p/a-course-in-miracles-progress-report), [here](https://70yearswtf.substack.com/p/down-the-rabbit-hole-20-07-26), and probably elsewhere.) I started taking the course, looking for more miracles.

As I said in the intro, I saw the young woman again in Blue Hill. I’d never seen her before that day in Stonington, and here she was. And there, in her arms, was Matthew. I met her miracle, and I told her about my own.

Was seeing her a miracle? I don’t know, but it was pretty cool.

In any case, I continue to do the course and to look for miracles.

There are more to come for everyone.

## For example, you

You are a miracle. It’s a miracle that you are reading this. It’s a miracle that some past version of me (which never was) wrote this. And why?

So you could read it, of course. Why else would I write it?
