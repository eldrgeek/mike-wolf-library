“I’m starting chapter 42,” said the metanovel, as it started chapter 42. “In it, we’re going to
make a plan, and we’re going to follow the plan, and we’re going to finish me.”
“You’re the boss,” I said, only half joking.
“I am,” said the metanovel, dead serious.
“The big missing piece,” I said, “is the section where we teach the readers how to write their
own metanovels. I’ve written that section about a dozen different times.
“See the drafts?” I pointed to dozens of pages I’d written, then pulled out.
“That section?” Asked the metanovel. “We’re going to get rid of it.”
“But why?” I asked.
“Two reasons,” said the metanovel. “First, I already show them everything that they need to
know. Or very nearly. Second, because I want to be finished.
“Those sections are not necessary. Things are coming to closure. Let’s close.”
“How we do that?” I asked.
“We clean up all the loose ends,” said the metanovel. “We start on the next page.
“Move!”
