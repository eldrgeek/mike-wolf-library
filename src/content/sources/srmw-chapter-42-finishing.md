<p>“I’m starting chapter 42,” said the metanovel, as it started chapter 42. “In it, we’re going to make a plan, and we’re going to follow the plan, and we’re going to finish me.”</p>

<p>“You’re the boss,” I said, only half joking.</p>

<p>“I am,” said the metanovel, dead serious.</p>

<p>“The big missing piece,” I said, “is the section where we teach the readers how to write their own metanovels. I’ve written that section about a dozen different times.</p>

<p>“See the drafts?” I pointed to dozens of pages I’d written, then pulled out.</p>

<p>“That section?” Asked the metanovel. “We’re going to get rid of it.”</p>

<p>“But why?” I asked.</p>

<p>“Two reasons,” said the metanovel. “First, I already show them everything that they need to know. Or very nearly. Second, because I want to be finished.</p>

<p>“Those sections are not necessary. Things are coming to closure. Let’s close.”</p>

<p>“How we do that?” I asked.</p>

<p>“We clean up all the loose ends,” said the metanovel. “We start on the next page.</p>

<p>“Move!”</p>
