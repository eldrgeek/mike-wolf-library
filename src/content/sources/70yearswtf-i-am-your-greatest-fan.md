No, it’s not a typo.

When My man Moses asked, “What is your name?” that was My answer.

I AM.

I AM your greatest fan.

“My greatest fan?” I imagined an [imaginary reader](https://70yearswtf.substack.com/p/imaginary-readers-imaginary-god) asking. “Or Michael’s greatest fan?”

“Or my greatest fan?” I imagined another imaginary reader asking.

“I AM, your greatest fan,” comes the answer to all, “because I AM.”

My man Jesus told you that God is love and IAM love.

I AM, is in you, and of you, and for you.

All of you.

I AM, is in every brother and every sister, every fish, and every tree.

“In me?” I imagined a mountain asking.

“I AM,” comes the reply.

I AM, the greatest fan of all My children. I play no favorites. I love every child.

I AM, the Source of Love, Forgiveness, Faith, and Hope.

I have [many Names and even more names](https://70yearswtf.substack.com/p/who-is-fafkag). I have a plan, every plan, [and all the plans](https://70yearswtf.substack.com/p/gods-plans-and-the-multiverse).

Call on Me, and I AM there.

I AM here.

I AM there.

And like Roy Kent

I AM.
