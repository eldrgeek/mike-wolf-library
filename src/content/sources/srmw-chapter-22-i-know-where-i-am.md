<p>I looked around me in wonder. The world had changed.</p>

<p>“Alright,” I said to God. “I know where I am. I’m at the center of the universe.”</p>

<p>“That’s what I always told you, Michael,” said Mom.</p>

<p>“Yes, Mom,” I said, “but you meant it figuratively, not literally. And you said I was the center of Dad’s universe. God’s telling me that I am at the center of <em>the</em> universe. The whole universe. And so is everyone else.”</p>

<p>“That’s right,” said God. “Now how can you not enjoy life when I’ve made you the center of all of creation?”</p>

<p>“You might be taking some liberties with the physics,” I said to God, after I’d done some more research, “but damn! It feels good to stand here and <em>feel</em> the universe all around me.”</p>

<p>“God can take all the liberties She wants to,” said my mother. “She’s God. That’s even better than being QFE.”</p>

<p>“What’s QFE,” asked the metanovel.</p>

<p>“It was my mother’s title before she died,” I answered. “Mom was Queen of Fucking Everything.”</p>

<p>“Was then, am now,” said Mom. “I’m just DQFE.”</p>

<p>I turned back to God. “It’s pretty cool,” I said.</p>

<p>“It’s more than just cool,” God said. “Now that you know where you are, in the center of the universe, let’s work on understanding what you are.”</p>

<p>“Alright,” I said.</p>

<p>“In the next chapter,” God said.</p>
