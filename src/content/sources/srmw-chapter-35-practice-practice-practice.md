<p>Practice! I reminded myself. Practice. Practice. Practice. Practice. Practice. Practice. Practice. Practice.</p>

<p>I kept typing the word ‘practice.’ That was what I needed to do. Not remember. Not be reminded. Practice.</p>

<p>Practice. Practice. Practice.</p>

<p>Here it was late at night, and I was practicing. And I was going to go to sleep shortly. And in the morning I would practice. Practice. Practice.</p>

<p>Practice. Practice. Practice. I’d known the answer for a long time. Practice. Practice. Practice. Practice. Practice.</p>

<p>Practice was the answer.</p>

<p>The author was right.</p>

<p>He’d written about his inability to practice, and was practicing by writing about his inability to practice, but he still didn’t understand that he still didn’t understand how to practice.</p>

<p>But practice would lead him to understanding.</p>
