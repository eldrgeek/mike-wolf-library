<p>Two days later I wrote this chapter.</p>

<p>“This is not the way to practice practicing,” said the metanovel.</p>

<p>“It’s not,” agreed my computer.</p>

<p>“Really,” said the doorknob, exasperated. “The answer is so obvious.”</p>

<p>I thought for a while.</p>

<p>Then I sat down.</p>

<p>My computer and I typed: “You’re right. It’s obvious.”</p>
