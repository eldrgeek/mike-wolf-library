<p><em>I give up!</em> He thought.</p>

<p>“I give up!” He said, for emphasis.</p>

<p>“I give up!!” He said, with two exclamation marks, for even more emphasis.</p>

<p>I’ve got to face reality. My writing is crap. My only hope is the muse.</p>

<p>I’m going to trust her.</p>

<p>I’m gong to do what she says!</p>

<p>I’m going to delete every copy of everything that I ever wrote.</p>

<p>I’m going to start fresh.</p>

<p>I’m really excited about the chance to work with a real muse.</p>

<p>I can’t wait for New Chapter 1.</p>
