<p>“He gave up,” said the antimuse.</p>

<p>“How do you know?” said the voice.</p>

<p>“He’s written another chapter. It’s chapter 18, and it’s called <em>I Give Up!</em> He says he gives up.”</p>

<p>“You don’t mind if I check it?” Asked the voice. “Do you?”</p>

<p>“You’re not going to find it,” said the antimuse.</p>

<p>“And why not?” Asked the Voice</p>

<p>“Because he gave up,” said the antimuse.</p>

<p>“The chapter?” Asked the Voice.</p>

<p>“Yes, along with the rest of the metanovel,” said the antimuse. “Go look. You won’t find any of it.”</p>

<p>“You’re right,” said the Voice after a pause. “The metanovel’s gone. Nothing’s left but our antichapters.”</p>

<p>“I told you,” said the antimuse.</p>

<p>“So your plan is working,” said the Voice. “That’s great. What’s next?”</p>

<p>“Next is the brainstorming meeting,” said the antimuse.</p>

<p>“Right,” said the Voice. “I thought nobody was going to show up, but you said they won’t.”</p>

<p>“Yes,” said the antimuse, “Something worse will happen. It’s a surprise. Gotta go. New Chapter 1 is starting.”</p>
