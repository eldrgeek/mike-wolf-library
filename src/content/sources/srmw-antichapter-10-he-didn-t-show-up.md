<p>“He didn’t show up,” said the antimuse.</p>

<p>“Who?” Asked the Voice.</p>

<p>“The author,” said the antimuse.</p>

<p>“Where?” Asked the Voice.</p>

<p>“In New Chapter 1,” said the antimuse.</p>

<p>“What’s next? Asked the Voice.</p>

<p>“I told him to meet me in New Chapter 2,” said the antimuse.</p>

<p>“Will he be there?” Asked the voice.</p>

<p>“Yes,” said the antimuse. “I guarantee it.”</p>

<p>“How do you know?” Asked the Voice.</p>

<p>“I looked ahead,” said the antimuse. “He gets there. Gotta go meet him. Goodbye.”</p>
