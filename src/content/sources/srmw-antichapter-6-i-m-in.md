<p>The same old phone rang. The same old hand picked it up.</p>

<p>“Hello,” said the same old Voice.</p>

<p>“Can’t talk,” said the antimuse. “I’m between chapters. He thinks he’s evolved a muse, but he’s evolved me. I’m in. And I’m starting to carry out the plan.”</p>

<p>“Good, good!” said the voice. “What’s the next step?”</p>

<p>“Can’t tell you right now,” said the antimuse. “Gotta go now. Next chapter’s already starting.”</p>

<p>“Goodbye,” said the voice. But the antimuse was already gone.</p>
