I’ve written this blog since I was 70. Now, (as I write this) I’m 82[1](#footnote-1). And every day, older.

For the first many years, it was a write-only blog. Not even I read it My kids might sometimes have read it, but I didn’t care[2](#footnote-2). I was only in it for the writing.

Eventually, I started reading what I had written. And I liked it. I still do. Those [Past Me’s](https://70yearswtf.substack.com/p/thank-you-past-me-thank-you-random-17-07-0)[3](#footnote-3) were quite the dudes. I hope the [Future Me’s](https://70yearswtf.substack.com/p/thank-you-past-me-thank-you-random-17-07-01) (same link) feels likewise and appreciates [Present Me](https://70yearswtf.substack.com/p/thank-you-past-me-thank-you-random-17-07-01) (same, again.) And by the way, [this post](https://70yearswtf.substack.com/p/thank-you-past-me-thank-you-random-17-07-01) (same link) was a game-changer for me.

## Heaven with slower internet

Now, I’m [living in the afterlife](https://70yearswtf.substack.com/p/even-more-like-heaven-and-the-internet), which I have described as “[like heaven, but with slower internet.](https://70yearswtf.substack.com/p/even-more-like-heaven-and-the-internethttps://70yearswtf.substack.com/p/even-more-like-heaven-and-the-internet)”

## The joys and woes of Substack

I moved from [Google’s Blogspo](https://70yearsoldwtf.blogspot.com)t to Substack in 2021. After several years, I tried adding

buttons. I got a few subscribers[4](#footnote-4) You’d think that would be cool. But, as I wrote in [this “draft” blog post](https://70yearswtf.substack.com/p/draft-having-subscribers-is-a-pain?utm_source=publication-search) having subscribers  [was a pain in the ass.](https://70yearswtf.substack.com/p/draft-having-subscribers-is-a-pain?utm_source=publication-search) (same link)

I became self-conscious about my writing. What if what I wrote didn’t meet the standard set by whatever I’d written that inspired someone to sign up? What if I disappointed people by not posting enough? What if I was losing it? What was the “it” that I was losing?

## Something changed

But something changed. My mind, of course. What else can change?

And [I went on a Hero’s Journey](https://70yearswtf.substack.com/p/the-heros-journey-continues) and stopped trying to play it both ways: trying to be visible and invisible.

I’ve decided to be visible.

I like the shit I write.

I’ve decided to make it visible.

I’m not “self-” promoting because I may have [no self.](https://70yearswtf.substack.com/p/avoiding-the-core-teachings-of-the-17-10-24?utm_source=publication-search)

I’m going to write more.

There’s the book I wrote in 2021, “Self-Referential Metanovel Writing for Dummies.”  I’m going to edit it, republish it, and promote it.

It needs visibility.

“Finally!” says [The God I Don’t Believe in](https://70yearswtf.substack.com/p/the-god-i-dont-believe-in), who has helped me write the book and everything else I write. “Finally!”

Some people will like what I write. If that’s you, and you’re not a subscriber:
