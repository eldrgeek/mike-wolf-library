G*d: knock knock
Me: Who's there?.
G*d: I am.
Me: I am who?
G*d: I am who I am.

In case you don't get the joke: "I am who I am" is [Exodus 3:14](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Exodus%203%3A14&version=NIV). When Moses asks "Who are you" that's the answer. It is sometimes translated as "[I am that I am](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Exodus%203%3A14&version=KJV)"

This is why G*d (and I) thought the joke was funny.

Do you think it's funnier now that I have explained it?

No?

I guess you had to be there.

In the dream, G*d said She liked "who I am" better, plus, bonus, "it fits the knock-knock joke schema."

Or maybe I just made that up.
