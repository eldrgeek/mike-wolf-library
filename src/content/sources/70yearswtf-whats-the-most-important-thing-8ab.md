“Not another fucking War and Peace!” Shouted a reader. Not just one reader. All of them. OK, maybe ten. “Wasn’t one epic enough!”

“I’m going to keep this one short,” I promised.

“You’d better,” said some readers.

“Stop adding words,” I said.

## The most important thing

The most important thing is paying attention to the most important thing.

“Is this some of your meta meta crap?” Asks a reader. Probably Mira. But maybe you.

“Sort of, but not really,” I say, cryptically.

If you (meaning me) are not paying attention to the most important thing as the most important thing then it’s not the most important thing anymore. It’s just “The thing I am doing.”

Like this blog post.

The most important thing right now is paying attention to finishing it AND PUBLISHING IT while I am writing it.

The most important thing is being aware of anything that replaces paying attention to the most important thing,

Anything.

“Got it,” says a reader. “Now are you going to say the same fucking thing two dozen times in three hundred different ways?”

“No,” I say. “I’m generating an image and publishing.”

“Thank God!” says a reader.

“You’re welcome,” says God.[1](#footnote-1)
