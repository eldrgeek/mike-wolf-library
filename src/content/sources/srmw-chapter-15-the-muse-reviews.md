<p>“Inspire me,” I said to the muse. “Help me finish my metanovel. And make it really good.”</p>

<p>“Give me a copy of what you’ve written so far,” said the muse. “I have to have context. If I inspire you out of context my inspiration might suck.”</p>

<p>“Sure,” I said. “Do you want me to include this chapter?”</p>

<p>“Sure,” she said. “End it, and give the whole package to me.”</p>

<p>“When will you read it?” I asked.</p>

<p>“I’ll be done by Chapter 17,” She promised. “I’m a fast reader.”</p>
