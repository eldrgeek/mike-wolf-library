Suddenly I stood in the autumn cold in a wooded area near a trail. The leaves had all fallen.
The air was crisp. I knew what was coming because I’d lived it.
A young man, looking worried, ran past me. He continued for about a quarter mile beyond,
then stopped. He looked around, shouted something, then ran another quarter mile up the road,
and out of sight. A few minutes later I saw him running back. He passed me again, and continued
in the other direction, running until I could no longer see him. In a few minutes he ran back. I
looked at his face as he approached. He was crying. He looked like shit. He was me.
This is too much, I thought. I’m not ready for this. Not nearly. I’ll write it later.
