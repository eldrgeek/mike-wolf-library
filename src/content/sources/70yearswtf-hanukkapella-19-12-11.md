## Dreidel

For those who don’t know how to play [Dreidel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dreidel), and can’t decode the symbols on the faces:

- If נ (*nun*) is facing up, the player does nothing.
- If ג (*gimel*) is facing up, the player gets everything in the pot.
- If ה (*hei*) is facing up, the player receives/gets half of the pieces in the pot. If there are an odd number of pieces in the pot, the player takes half the pot rounded up to the nearest whole number.
- If ש (*shin*) or פ (*pe*) is facing up, the player adds one of their game pieces to the pot (sometimes accompanied by the chant “shin, shin, put one in”[[19]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dreidel#cite_note-20)). In some game versions, a Shin results in adding three game pieces to the pot, one for each stem of the letter shin (ש).
