<p>And a new one began.</p>

<p>The bar disappeared. We were in the back seat of a car, a Nissan Maxima, driving along the Mass Turnpike, approaching Natick.</p>

<p>“That’s me in the front seat, isn’t it?” I asked God.</p>

<p>“Yes,” She said.</p>

<p>“I don’t look too happy, do I?” I asked.</p>

<p>“F*** no.”</p>

<p>“What’s going on?” I asked.</p>

<p>“See if you can remember,” God said.</p>

<p>In a moment it all came back to me. I was driving to my job. After what had been the best year of my life, things had started to turn to shit, and I was in now starting one of the worst years of my life.</p>

<p>“I can remember,” I said. “This is something I’d rather forget.”</p>

<p>“Remember!” God commanded. She was serious.</p>

<p>“Alright,” I said. I didn’t want to, but I was remembering. It was all coming back to me. It sucked.</p>

<p>“Now here’s the trick,” said God. “Can you en-joy what’s going on?”</p>

<p>“What the f*** am I supposed to enjoy?” I yelled angrily at God.</p>

<p>“Find something a***hole!” God yelled back. No matter how loud you can yell, God can yell a lot louder than you. I did what She said.</p>

<p>I looked. And I found something. The sunlight. Even though my life sucked that day, it sucked on a beautiful day. If I couldn’t enjoy my life, I could at least en-joy my day. I recalled the remembered day and put joy into my experience of that day.</p>

<p>I had my health, too. That was something. I wasn’t dying like my friend Tommy would be in a few years. I wasn’t starving. There were a lot of things that I could use to enjoy that day. So I did. I appreciated the things that were good and put more joy into the day.</p>

<p>I was at the center of the universe. However much things sucked, that was an unalterable fact. I spread my awareness out, anchored myself as far as my mind could reach. I felt calm. I added to the joy I felt.</p>

<p>And I was a mistake who had made mistakes. The anger I’d felt, mostly directed at myself, began to evaporate. It wasn’t so serious. More joy.</p>

<p>And I was surrounded by family. With my awareness spread I felt the life around me. Trees and birds. Cousins. Everything connected. I wasn’t doing so well, but most of my family was doing great. It felt good.</p>

<p>“Good start,” said God. “Now let’s try something a little harder. Let’s look at the worst day in your life.”</p>

<p>“I don’t like that idea,” I said. “I really don’t like it at all.”</p>

<p>“Have a little faith,” said God.</p>
