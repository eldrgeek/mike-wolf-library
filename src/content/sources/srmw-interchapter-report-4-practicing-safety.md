<p>And suddenly he was back in the safe place he’d found in Chapter 30, <em>Outside Space And Time.</em></p>

<p>He imagined that he was far away from his life’s turmoil, but still at the center at the universe.</p>

<p>He imagined the universe surrounding him.</p>

<p>He imagined himself as a mistake.</p>

<p>He imagined himself connected to his family. Some of his relatives were struggling, but most were just working. All connected.</p>

<p>He imagined that he knew what he needed to practice to face that day. And he knew.</p>

<p>He imagined that he had the time he needed to practice what he needed to practice to face that day.</p>

<p>He took the time and practiced.</p>

<p>And practiced.</p>

<p>And practiced.</p>

<p>He was between chapters so he could practice all he wanted.</p>
