[Everything is an idea](https://70yearswtf.substack.com/p/everything-is-an-idea?utm_source=publication-search). Everything is full of ideas [I am an idea](https://70yearswtf.substack.com/p/i-am-an-idea-part-1-its-all-about?utm_source=publication-search), overflowing with ideas.

Some of the ideas within me are stories, because I am full of stories—just like you.

Other ideas are just ideas.

I am grateful for the ideas that come to me. Like everything else, ideas arise and they pass away.

I want to do right by the ideas that arise. 

But in my efforts to do right, I have repeatedly made an error.

## The error

I try to make ideas perfect before I put them out into the world. 

Often I have failed to put them out of the world, because I believed that they were not perfect the way they came to me or the way I first represented (re-presented) them.

I am now determined to take each idea, translate it into some reasonable representation, like a blog post, and put it out in the world.

I will make clear that the ideas are not “mine.” No one owns an idea—IP law notwithstanding.

Ideas are simply themselves.

I will try make it as easy for someone else to take ideas forward or come up with a variant. I will construct no barriers.

Once I put the idea into the world, it’s up to the idea to find someone who wants to take it further.

I’ve done my job.

You do yours.
