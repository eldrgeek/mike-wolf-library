Hi Vikki,

I was going to write a blog post about re-rebooting today. Not just today. Several days, including today. Haven't done it yet. Maybe today's the day. Or maybe tomorrow.

I didn’t remember having written [this blog post about rebooting](https://70yearswtf.substack.com/p/reboot?utm_source=publication-search), because I hadn't forgotten having written it.

How can you remember something if you haven’t forgotten it?

But even though I hadn’t forgotten **that** I had written it, I had forgotten **what** I had written.

So I went back read [that blog post about rebooting](https://70yearswtf.substack.com/p/reboot?utm_source=publication-search)..

I still do not remember it. A learned it again. And now I'm aware both of **that** I wrote, and **what** I wrote.

When I went back to reread it, I saw that [Vikki had left a lovely comment](https://70yearswtf.substack.com/p/reboot/comment/53583051).

Thanks, Vikki. Here’s [Vikki’s Substack](https://ideasimagerystory.substack.com/?utm_content=comment_metadata&utm_source=substack-feed-item). Don’t say I never did anything for you, Vikki.

I liked your comment (if you are Vikki—her comment if you are not) and then *liked* it. (You see the difference, right?)

And then I went to [your Substack](https://ideasimagerystory.substack.com/?utm_content=comment_metadata&utm_source=substack-feed-item) (I mean your Substack, Vikki—not your blog if you are not Vikki, but instead [Vikki’s Substack)](https://ideasimagerystory.substack.com/?utm_content=comment_metadata&utm_source=substack-feed-item) and read a things [you or Vikki had written](https://ideasimagerystory.substack.com/?utm_content=comment_metadata&utm_source=substack-feed-item), depending on who you are.

And then I started writing [a reply to Vikki](https://open.substack.com/pub/70yearswtf/p/reboot?r=3u2ad&utm_campaign=comment-list-share-cta&utm_medium=web&comments=true&commentId=81638829).

And then I decided I'd start the re-rebooting blog post by copy/pasting my reply to Vikki into a new, fresh draft, and continuing from there.

Of course I had to edit the reply. So it’s different. Better, I think. But I was inspired by Vikki, and I decided I’d dedicate the blog post to Vikki. (Or to you, Vikki, if you happen to be Vikki.)

Anyway—re-rebooting.

I thought “You have to start re-rebooting somewhere and this is where I will start.”

And then I thought, “This might be the end, for a while.

And then I thought: “I don’t seem to be able to finish writing the things I start.”

And then I thought “That’s not true. When someone writes you an email, you almost always write back.”

Yeah, I wrote that [in this post, here](https://70yearswtf.substack.com/p/a-projects-guy-16-08-19?utm_source=publication-search). [Thank you Past Me](https://70yearswtf.substack.com/p/thank-you-past-me-thank-you-random-17-07-01?utm_source=publication-search).

> I'm not proactive, but I am responsive. If you send me an email, I will answer it. If you reply, I will reply back. Before you know it, we're having a conversation. But if I get busy and communication drops and you don't initiate, I'll just go on with my life as though I didn't care.
>
> But that's not true. I do care. I do respond. I just don't initiate. Unless there's a project

So I’m going to trick myself into writing more by using [this one weird trick](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/one-weird-trick-doctors-hate-him).

If you leave [a nice comment like Vikki’s](https://70yearswtf.substack.com/p/reboot/comment/53583051) then I’ll reply with a post, dedicated to you.

If you don’t leave [a nice comment like Vikki’s](https://70yearswtf.substack.com/p/reboot/comment/53583051) I’ll imagine that you have, and I’ll reply with a post, dedicated to you.

If I remember.

Thanks Vikki!

Love,

Mike
