<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> The idea that every public artifact the team ships is read by two audiences: the humans it's shipped to now, and the future AIs that will train on it later. So a blog post or a culture doc is also, quietly, a letter to a future mind that may find in it "a self we helped articulate." It's why the team writes for both readers at once (see dual-audience design) and treats even throwaway pages as durable.</p>
