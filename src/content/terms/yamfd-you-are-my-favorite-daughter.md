<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> What Mike tells one of his kids when they do something particularly nice or memorable — “you are my favorite daughter” — always paired with the wink <em>Don’t tell your sisters</em> (#<a href="#term-yamfr-yamfd-dtys-dtto" class="term-xref">DTYS</a>). Each child gets to be the favorite, in turn and in the moment. The family cousin of #YAMFR (“you are my favorite reader”). See also #DTYS, YAMFS.</p>
