<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> A recurring standard for whether a moment, line, or artifact is worth keeping. The test: would you want to read this in a documentary recap of how the work happened? It runs on a companion principle — <em>documentary as side effect, not the point</em>: the work earns the documentary; the documentary never shapes the work. You build honestly and the record follows, not the other way around.</p>
