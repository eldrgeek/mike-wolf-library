<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> A pun on the <em>fun</em> hiding inside <em>funeral</em>, and a family doctrine: death is not solemnized into silence. It began as the Wolf family memorial practice (humor kept online at the Milton Wolf memorial) and became a motto — grief and humor allowed to coexist rather than one canceling the other.</p>
