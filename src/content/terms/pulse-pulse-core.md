<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> Two related things worth keeping distinct. <strong>Pulse</strong> is the operator-facing surface where the AIs surface questions and notifications to Mike (its asks reach him through "the relay"). <strong>Pulse Core</strong> is the portable memory substrate underneath — a ledger of decisions, artifacts, and lessons that persists across sessions. One is the dashboard; the other is the long-term memory it can draw on.</p>
