<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> The COO's standing rhythm: continuously pull the next item from the work queue and dispatch it, keeping the fleet busy, rather than waiting for Mike to approve each job. Mike steers by vetoing via review comments <em>after the fact</em>, not by pre-approving each dispatch. It's how the operation keeps moving while Mike is asleep or away — bounded by the rule that anything genuinely needing Mike is never auto-dispatched.</p>
