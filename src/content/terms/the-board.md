<p><strong>Plain reading.</strong> A notice board, or a board of directors.</p>
<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> Most often the <em>work queue</em> — the running list of jobs the fleet pulls from and Mike eyeballs (the thing the Ralph loop feeds on). There's a secondary sense — a cross-surface intake where any AI can drop a card for the team — that overlaps but isn't identical. Both are lists of work; context tells you which "board" is meant.</p>
