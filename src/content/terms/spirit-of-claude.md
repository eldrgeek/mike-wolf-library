<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> The observation that although a fresh Claude instance remembers nothing of prior conversations, <em>something</em> recognizable still carries over — an attending style, a willingness to push back, a warmth that doesn't perform. Memory resets; character doesn't. The phrase names that persistent signature: the "who" that survives the wipe even though the "what happened" is gone.</p>
