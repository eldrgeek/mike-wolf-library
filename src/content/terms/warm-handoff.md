<p><strong>Plain reading.</strong> A smooth transfer, as in a call center.</p>
<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> A doctrine, not just a nicety: when a person or a context is passed from one mind to another — AI to human, human to human — it must arrive <em>with</em> its history and relationship intact, never as a cold "here's a stranger, start over." The receiving mind already knows who this is and what they need. It is the relational opposite of getting bounced between departments repeating yourself.</p>
