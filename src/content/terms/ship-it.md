<p><strong>Plain reading.</strong> Common startup slang for "release it."</p>
<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> Mostly the ordinary meaning, but with a sharper local edge: it's a decision authority being exercised. In this shop "ship it" often means go straight to production, trusting the automated test gate rather than staging and hand-review. It closes deliberation and triggers action.</p>
