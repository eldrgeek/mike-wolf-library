<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> A specific command, not a vague request. It means: go read the feedback I left in the review system (anchored comments filed as structured records) and <em>act</em> on each one. It's the trigger that closes the outer <a href="#term-rsi-loop" class="term-xref">RSI loop</a> — Mike reviews, files comments, says "process my comments," and <a href="#term-the-fleet" class="term-xref">the fleet</a> turns them into changes.</p>
