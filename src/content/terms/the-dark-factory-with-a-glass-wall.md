<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> The build side of SOMA. "Dark factory" borrows the manufacturing term for a lights-out plant that runs without humans on the floor: AI agents do the mechanical building out of the user's direct path. The "glass wall" is the crucial addition — humans can <em>see</em> status, intent, reasoning, and previews at any time, without being forced to read code diffs. The human supervises meaning and risk; the machines do the assembly and leave their reasoning inspectable. (Mike also uses "the dark factory" loosely for his whole agent build workflow.)</p>
