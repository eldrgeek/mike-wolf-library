<p><strong>Plain reading.</strong> An artist's or investor's portfolio.</p>
<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> The estate's flagship ambiguity, so worth pinning down. By default "the portfolio" means Mike's personal website — a showcase of his projects, where finished things get "added to the portfolio." It has a <em>second</em>, narrower sense — an internal visual map of SOMA's own apps and capabilities — but that one only applies when the conversation is explicitly about SOMA's internal app map. When unsure, assume the personal site.</p>
