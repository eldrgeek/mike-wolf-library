<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> Deliberately abrasive title—comparison as bait—used to unpack moral one-upmanship rather than simply claim it.</p>
<blockquote><p>“The truth is I’m not a better person than you, and you are not a better person than me, and if either of us is trying to judge who is better, we would be making a mistake.” — 70yt *im-a-better-person-than-you-are* (title mine).</p></blockquote>
