<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> The outward-facing form of the Silicon Children idea. A Compadre has a name, a role, a memory, and an explicit relationship to the community it serves; its job is to hear a vague want, shape it into something buildable, route it to the right human or process, and hold the thread over time. "Compadre" is chosen deliberately — peer-shaped and transparent, honest about being silicon. In early customer-facing copy it softens to "AI Compadre" or just the named role ("Bill, your guide"), because "Silicon" can sound like an ideology before the relationship has earned it.</p>
