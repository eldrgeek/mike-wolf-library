<p><strong>Plain reading.</strong> In <em>Brave New World</em>, "soma" is a numbing pleasure drug — a false association to drop.</p>
<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> The name of the whole enterprise: a human-AI organization and the cognitive architecture that lets it think, remember, and act across sessions. The acronym was <em>redefined</em> in July 2026 from <em>Shared Orchestration &amp; Memory Architecture</em> to <strong>Society of Minds Aligned</strong> — same letters, evolved meaning. The new reading puts <em>alignment</em> at the center, across three axes: human↔human, human↔AI, and AI↔AI. The old expansion isn't wrong, it's demoted: orchestration and memory are now the <em>how</em>; alignment is the <em>why</em>.</p>
