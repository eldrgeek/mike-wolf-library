<p><strong>Plain reading.</strong> To send off, as in shipping goods or emergency services.</p>
<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> The core verb of the operation: to delegate a self-contained task to a worker AI that runs it to completion and reports back, rather than doing it in the current conversation. It comes in flavors — a background worker via the `cc-dispatch` tool, or "dispatch to Mac / dispatch to VPS," an email-as-transport trick where any Claude surface emails a special address with a `[DISPATCH:Mac]` subject line to trigger execution on that machine. The manager's stance is "dispatch, don't micromanage."</p>
