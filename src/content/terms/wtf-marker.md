<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> A specific signal, not just profanity. When Mike begins a message with "WTF," it means the team did something in a previous turn that genuinely stunned him (good or bad) — and that exchange gets routed automatically to a public "WTF" feed as documentary content. The letters are the trigger; the surprise is the point.</p>
