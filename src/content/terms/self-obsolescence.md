<p><strong>Plain reading.</strong> Becoming outdated — usually a bad thing.</p>
<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> Here it's the <em>aim</em>, stated with pride. The long-term goal of the whole build effort is to empower non-technical people to make their own tools until Mike himself is no longer the bottleneck or the necessary expert. Success looks like being needed less. A builder deliberately engineering away the need for the builder.</p>
