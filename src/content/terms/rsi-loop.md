<p><strong>Plain reading.</strong> In medicine, "RSI" is repetitive strain injury — nothing to do with it. (And beware: one early transcript mis-glossed it as "Requirement/Issue/Idea" — also wrong.)</p>
<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> The self-improvement cycle every SOMA app is meant to have. An <strong>inner loop</strong>: produce something, have it automatically reviewed/judged, diff the defects, fix the specialist that produced them, produce again. And an <strong>outer loop</strong>: real users give conversational feedback that gets shaped into coherent development requests. The two loops together are how an app is supposed to get better on its own between human check-ins.</p>
