<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> A hard-won rule. A tool returning `ok: true` does not mean the work happened — one night five workers all reported success and all silently produced nothing. So "done" is only "done" when you've confirmed the real output has substance (files exist and contain what they should), not when a status code says so. Distrust the receipt; inspect the goods.</p>
