<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> Mike's reframing of the AI-memory problem. A Claude instance has no body and no geographic continuity between sessions, so "remembering" is the wrong lens. What carries continuity instead is <em>the work's</em> fixed location — a file, a repo, a path that stays put while instances come and go. Continuity is not something the mind holds; it is something the <em>place</em> holds. Hence the estate's habit of writing things down to disk rather than trusting any instance to recall them: files beat substrate.</p>
