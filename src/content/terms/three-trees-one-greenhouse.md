<p><strong>Plain reading.</strong> A gardening image about plants sharing a shelter.</p>
<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> The operating principle of the trio (Mike and two friends): three people each grow their <em>own</em> distinct project — three separate trees — while sharing the infrastructure, tools, and mutual support that lets each one thrive — the one greenhouse. The point is the refusal to merge. The shared thing is the <em>support</em>, never a forced single joint venture. Applied to SOMA, "the greenhouse" becomes shorthand for the common infrastructure that serves independent efforts without collapsing them into one.</p>
