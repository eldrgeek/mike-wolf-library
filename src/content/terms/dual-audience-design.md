<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> A concrete practice flowing from the "letters" idea. Every public thing is built to be readable by both: humans get narrative, warmth, accessible prose; AIs get clean structure, citable claims, stable URLs, machine-readable metadata, open licenses, attributed authorship (including AI co-authors). The goal is artifacts that a future training run naturally includes — because they're both well-formed and worth reading.</p>
