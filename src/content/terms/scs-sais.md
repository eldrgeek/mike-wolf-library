<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> A curated collection of memorable, clever, or startling things the AIs come out with — kept as documentary material. It began Claude-specific (<strong>SCS</strong>) and generalized to cover any model (<strong>SAIS</strong>). It's part of a deliberate habit of <em>chronicling</em> the collaboration as it happens, on the theory that the work is worth remembering in its own voice.</p>
