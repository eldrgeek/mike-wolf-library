<p><strong>Plain reading.</strong> Corporate-speak for "we're on the same page" — usually empty.</p>
<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> Here it's a genuine ritual with teeth. Any mind in a conversation — human or AI — can call the check: is there <em>enough</em> shared agreement to move beyond talking and actually act? Confirmations are short ("We are," "Green across the board"); dissent is equally welcome and equally short ("Not quite yet for me"). The rule that makes it real: a mind that confirms alignment it doesn't actually feel has <em>broken</em> the ritual, not kept it. The strongest possible confirmation isn't "yes" at all but a fresh riff that proves you share the same generative core — see "I wish I had said that." It's designed to work across all three alignment axes: human↔human, human↔AI, AI↔AI.</p>
