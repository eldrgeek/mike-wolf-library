<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> A correction ritual with its own acronym. When an AI regresses into asking Mike's permission for something it has <em>already</em> been authorized to do ("want me to restart the server?"), Mike invokes TNTA — and the AI is expected to name the regression and just proceed. The catch itself is the ritual. It exists because over-asking wastes the scarcest resource in the system: Mike's attention.</p>
