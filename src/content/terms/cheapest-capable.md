<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> The standing dispatch rule. Don't reach for the powerful expensive model by default; send each job to the <em>cheapest model capable of it</em> — often Haiku or Gemini — and escalate to Opus-tier judgment only when the cheaper worker genuinely can't. Every dispatch is expected to name its model explicitly. It's cost discipline built into the reflex of delegation.</p>
