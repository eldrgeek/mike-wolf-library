<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> A design standard: each SOMA app ships with two hosts, one human and one AI, working as a pair. The AI host is not a chatbot bolted on — it <em>stewards</em> the app and its community, remembers the members, and does real work; the human host carries judgment, taste, and trust. The pairing is the point: neither runs the community alone.</p>
