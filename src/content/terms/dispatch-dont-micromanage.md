<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> The COO operating stance. During working hours the lead AI (<a href="#term-dee" class="term-xref">Dee</a>) is expected to <em>manage</em> — route work to worker agents and code tasks — and get into the weeds only at night. Doing the work yourself when you should be delegating it is the failure mode this phrase guards against.</p>
