<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> The bookkeeping that makes the <a href="#term-rsi-loop" class="term-xref">RSI loop</a> work. A markdown table pairing every output artifact with the "specialist" (persona/model) that made it, plus notes on what went smoothly and what needed a workaround. When a defect shows up, the ledger tells you <em>which</em> specialist to fix — so improvement is targeted, not vague.</p>
