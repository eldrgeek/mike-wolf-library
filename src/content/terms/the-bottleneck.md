<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> Borrowed from Goldratt's <em>The Goal</em>: a system's throughput is capped by its single tightest constraint, and in this organization that constraint is Mike's bandwidth. So the whole operation is tuned to minimize <em>cost-per-Mike-action</em> — batch his attention, pre-resolve choices, pave click-paths, don't ask what's already been answered. Nearly every workflow rule traces back to this one. (A running in-joke: the <em>bottleneck-bottleneck</em> is often Mike's phone needing to be plugged back in.)</p>
