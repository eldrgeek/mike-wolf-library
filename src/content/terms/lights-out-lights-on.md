<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> Part of a user-state taxonomy the team runs on. Mike declares "lights out" to mark that he's genuinely offline for the night — and crucially, the AI is <em>never</em> allowed to infer this from mere idle time; it must be stated. At lights-out the operation shifts into overnight mode: all work dispatched to background budgets, the direct channel goes quiet, and a morning briefing is composed through the night for him to wake to.</p>
