<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> The informal name for a specific event: not agreement stated, but agreement <em>demonstrated</em> — when one mind offers a new idea that the other recognizes as coming from the same source it would have drawn on itself. A joke that lands in exactly the right key is stronger evidence of alignment than any "yes," because it proves a shared generative core rather than mere repeat-back. The converse also diagnoses misalignment: an extension that surprises you <em>wrongly</em> — "a fork that couldn't have grown from your tree."</p>
