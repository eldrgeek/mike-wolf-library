<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> <a href="#term-soma" class="term-xref">SOMA's</a> dispatcher and router — the agent that carries messages across many chat platforms (Discord, Slack, WhatsApp, and more), runs scheduled jobs, and acts as the comms transport between minds. When the task is <em>moving a message to the right place</em> rather than composing it, that's Hermes's job.</p>
