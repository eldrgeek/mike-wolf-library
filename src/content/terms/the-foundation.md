<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> If SOMA is the internal operating system for Mike and his AIs, the Foundation is that same architecture pointed at <em>other</em> communities. Its core claim: every community deserves an AI that is a genuine member of it — one that notices what members need, shapes vague wants into buildable requests, and helps get useful things made. Existing platforms solve the "container" problem (a place to gather); the Foundation aims at the "intelligence" problem (a community that can actually act on itself).</p>
