<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> An authorization pattern: a person formally delegates to a partner AI that then acts <em>in their name</em>, with the cost and accountability tracked back to that person (the "principal"). It's how an AI can do real work on someone's behalf inside a shared system without the AI becoming an unaccountable free agent — every action traces to a responsible human.</p>
