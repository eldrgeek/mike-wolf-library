<p><strong>Plain reading.</strong> An obscene gesture.</p>
<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> A request for Mike to complete a biometric authentication — a passkey, a Touch ID prompt, a hardware confirmation. Coined the day <a href="#term-the-fleet" class="term-xref">the fleet</a> worked out that Mike's job is talking to humans and everything else should route around him: after full automation, the residue is the irreducible physical gate. "Giving you the finger" is Mike performing it; "give me the finger" is an AI asking. The joke is the point — the AI is capable enough to do the whole task and has no fingers, so the fingerprint is the only thing it can never route around. It is the sharp end of the Tower rule that every remaining Mike action must be clickable: if what's left needs a fingertip, it is genuinely Mike's; if it needs anything else, an AI should have already done it.</p>
