<p><strong>Plain reading.</strong> A relay race baton, or an electrical switch.</p>
<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> A live piece of infrastructure — the message channel that connects the AIs' questions and notifications to where Mike actually sees them (and connects surfaces to each other). "The relay is down" means that channel has broken, so dispatched work can't reach him. One caution: the word points at two different real relays in the estate (the main dispatch/notification relay, and a separate one used for putting AI personas into live video calls) — context tells you which.</p>
