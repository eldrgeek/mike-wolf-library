<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> A hybrid discipline applied per-decision, not per-project. The structural, hard-to-change things — data models, auth boundaries — get carefully <em>specified</em> up front (waterfall-style). The surface things — UX, wording, developer-experience polish — get <em>improvised</em> and iterated (agile-style). Know which kind of decision you're making and apply the matching rigor. Over-specifying polish wastes time; under-specifying foundations causes damage.</p>
