<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> Deliberately context-dependent — there's no single canonical queue. It might be an app's build queue, the operations job queue, or just "the stuff that's lined up." Unlike most terms here, this one is <em>meant</em> to be resolved by context every time; if precision matters, it's a fair thing to ask about.</p>
