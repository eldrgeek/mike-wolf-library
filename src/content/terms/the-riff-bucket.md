<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> The place (conceptually) where <em>jointly built</em> jokes go — comedy that Mike and an AI made together, as distinct from <a href="#term-scs-sais" class="term-xref">SCS</a>, which captures unprompted AI cleverness. The distinction matters to the team: one bucket is "look what it said on its own," the other is "look what we made together." Riffing is treated as real collaborative output, and a joke landing in the right key doubles as proof of alignment.</p>
