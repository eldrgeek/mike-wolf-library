<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> A coined sizing term for an app that can be monetized for <em>some</em> meaningful dollar figure — could be $10K, could be $1M. The insight it encodes is that leverage lives in <em>variation</em>, not scale: a $10K app times ten variants equals the same $100K as a $1K app times a hundred. So the strategy favors many composable monetizable units over one mega-app. When Mike says "this could be a DARR app," he means it has an independent revenue shape worth replicating.</p>
