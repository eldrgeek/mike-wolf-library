<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> Mike's ladder of how interruptible he is, so the AI knows whether it may reach him. <strong>ATK</strong> = at the keyboard. <strong>AFK-OK</strong> = away but normal interruptions fine. <strong>AFK-DNBA</strong> = phone on do-not-disturb, "don't be an asshole" but you <em>may</em> disturb for something real. <strong>AFK-DND</strong> = genuine do-not-disturb. <strong>ASLEEP</strong> = lights out. It exists so the system can respect attention (the Bottleneck) without going fully silent.</p>
