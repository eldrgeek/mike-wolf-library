Years of research at Placebionix, the self-proclaimed world leader in the field, has produced the SuperPlacebo family. Placebionix uses a proprietary blend of hyperbole, amplification, and fabrication, which—according to customers—make SuperPlacebos the most effective placebos they have ever used.

Randomized double-blind placebo control studies (RDBPCS) have been the “Gold Standard” in intervention-based studies. In an RDBPCS, individuals are randomly assigned to treatment and control groups. Those in the treatment group get a treatment believed to improve the condition; those in the control group get a placebo—an inert substance or an intervention believed to be ineffective. In some cases, a third group is given neither treatment nor placebo but told to wait.

Placebo control studies are the standard because of the powerful “placebo effect,” which causes improvement even when there’s no mechanism for improvement.

The placebo effect is so powerful that even when patients are told that they are being given substances that do nothing, patients will show impressive signs of improvement.
