<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> A refrain Mike carries from Scott Alexander’s novel <em>Unsong</em>, where it is the running theological joke and thesis at once: in a created, meaning-saturated world there are no accidents. Mike uses it to mark the moments that arrive too aptly to be random.</p>
<blockquote><p>“It was not a coincidence, because nothing is ever a coincidence.” — Scott Alexander, <em>Unsong</em></p></blockquote>
