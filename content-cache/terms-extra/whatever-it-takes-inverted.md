<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> Inverts grit cliché: confession of impression-management — work hard at seeming to work hard, including convincing yourself. Ritual of exposure that may still be performance.</p>
<blockquote><p>“whatever it takes to do as little as possible to seem to have worked hard” / “I will do whatever it takes to convince people that I’m willing to do whatever it takes.” — 70yt *whatever-it-takes*; *whatever-it-takes-redux*.</p></blockquote>
