<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> Coined to parallel <em>SBM</em> (son-by-marriage). Mike leaves the F cheerfully unresolved — landing on “Daughters by <em>Fertilization</em>” while pointedly rejecting a bawdier candidate. The joke is the deliberate ambiguity; the affection underneath it is not ambiguous at all. See also SBM, YAMFS.</p>
