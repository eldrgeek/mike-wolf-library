<p><strong>What we mean.</strong> SBM = Sons by Marriage (not “son-in-law”); DBF = Daughters by Fertilization (awkward half of the pun). Family Tenure = permanent son status, no probation. ZNS = hierarchical IDs (Z1/Z2/Z3, Zbm, etc.). SRMW <em>Metanzygotes</em> foreshadows meta-offspring register.</p>
<blockquote><p>“The sons are SBM--Sons by Marriage.” / “I have given each one Family Tenure” / “the three Zygotes, and the Metanzygotes” — 70yt *sbm-and-dbf*, *zns17*, Heaven posts; SRMW dedication.</p></blockquote>
